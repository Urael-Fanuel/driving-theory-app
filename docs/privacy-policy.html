🔒 This app does not require registration or login. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

📋 Overview

መንጃ ፍቃድ በቀላል መንገድ ("Driving Theory in Simple Steps") is a free educational app that helps Ethiopians worldwide learn driving theory in Amharic. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we use it.

By using this app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📦 Data We Collect

Minimal Data Collection

We collect only the minimum data necessary for the app to function:

  • Anonymous User ID — A random identifier that is not linked to your name or contact details. It is used to save your learning progress and to connect it with the optional data described below.
  • Learning Progress — which signs and topics you have studied, quiz scores, and exam results. Stored locally on your device and optionally synced to our backend (Supabase) using only your anonymous ID.
  • Device Information — device type, operating system version, and app version. Used for crash reporting and compatibility checks only.
  • Location (optional) — If you allow it when the app asks, the app reads your phone’s location while the app is open (never in the background), turns it into a city name, and stores the coordinates and the city together with your anonymous ID. We use this only to choose which nearby driving instructors’ ads to show you and to count, per city, how many people saw or tapped each ad. If you have not been asked yet, the app may estimate only your city from your IP address and store that city (we do not store the IP address itself). If you decline, that estimate is deleted and not used again. You can refuse location and still use the whole app.
  • Diagnostic reports — When something goes wrong (for example a sound does not play, a download fails or the app crashes), the app may send a short technical report to our server. It contains the error message (with private parts of web addresses, keys, tokens and email addresses removed), the name of the audio file involved, your phone’s manufacturer and model, the Android and app versions, the type of network, whether you were online, and a random code for that session. A report may be linked to your anonymous ID. It does not contain your name, contact details, voice recordings or location.
  • Ad views, taps and calls (driving-instructor ads) — When a driving instructor’s ad is on your screen (at least half of it visible for one second), when you tap it, or when you press its “call” button, the app saves a short record: which ad, which of these three events, which screen of the app and which learning mode (audio-based or text-based), the time (set by our server), your anonymous ID, and a city. The city is added by our server from the city already stored for you (from your location if you allowed it, or the estimate described above if you have not been asked yet); if you declined location, no city is saved with these records. We use them only to show instructors totals: how many people saw and tapped their ad, per day and per city. The instructors do not receive your anonymous ID or any other personal data, and we do not record the phone call itself, only the press of the button.
  • Voice statistics — To check that our recorded voices work, the app counts, per day, how many sentences were read from a recording, how many with the live voice, and how many recordings could not be played. Only these counts are sent, together with the app version and whether your phone is Android or iOS. They are not linked to your anonymous ID and contain no text, no recordings and no location.
  • Microphone — Used only when you tap the microphone button to answer by voice. The recording is sent through our server to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text to recognise the number you said. We do not store recordings.
  • “Why?” explanations — When you ask the app to explain why an answer is right or wrong, the question and the answers are sent through our server to Google’s Gemini AI service, which writes the explanation. Your name and contact details are never part of this request.

📢 Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized ads based on your interests.

AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (resettable in your device settings)
  • Approximate location (IP-based, not GPS)
  • App usage data and interaction with ads

Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Your choices in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choice when you open the app, before any ad is requested. If you refuse, Google may still show limited ads. These are not personalised with your data, but Google still uses some data, such as your IP address, to deliver the ad and to prevent fraud (Google’s explanation of limited ads). You can change your choice at any time with the “ad privacy choices” button on the Progress screen, which is shown only where this applies.

Besides banners, the app may show a full-screen Google ad when you finish a subtopic or a topic quiz, never during a lesson, a question or an exam.

🚗 Driving-Instructor Ads

Besides Google ads, the app shows ads from local driving instructors. To choose which ones to show, we compare your saved city (or, if you allowed location, your coordinates, to find the nearest instructor within about 30 km) with the instructors’ cities. The instructors do not receive your location or any other data from the app.

The app counts how often each instructor ad is seen, tapped or called (see “Data We Collect”), so that instructors can be shown totals per day and per city.

🎙️ Microphone Permission

The app requests microphone access only for the voice-answer feature in Engine A (the audio-based learning engine). Voice audio is sent through our server to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text for recognition and is not stored or logged by us.

You can deny microphone permission and still use the full app — voice answering will simply be unavailable.

🌐 Internet Access

Internet access is used for:

  • Loading audio files and images for signs and topics (from Supabase Storage)
  • Syncing your learning progress to the cloud (anonymous, no login required)
  • Text-to-Speech (Google Cloud TTS) for Amharic voice explanations
  • Loading advertisements (Google AdMob)
  • Finding your city: from your location if you allow it, or estimated from your IP address if you have not been asked yet
  • Sending the question to Google’s Gemini AI when you ask for an explanation
  • Sending short diagnostic reports when something goes wrong
  • Saving ad view, tap and call records and the anonymous voice counts described above

🤝 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — anonymous data storage and backend (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob — advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Cloud Speech-to-Text — voice recognition (not stored)
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — Amharic audio generation
  • Google Maps Geocoding — turns your coordinates into a city name
  • ipapi.co — turns an IP address into an approximate city
  • Google Gemini — writes the “Why?” explanations

👶 Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 16 and above (minimum age for driving theory study in most countries). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

🔐 Data Security

All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Anonymous IDs are randomly generated and do not contain your name or contact details.

🗓️ How Long We Keep Data

  • Learning progress, the anonymous ID and location data are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Diagnostic reports are kept only as long as needed to find and fix problems, and are deleted on request.
  • If you decline location, any city that was estimated from your IP address is deleted and is not used again.
  • Voice recordings are not stored.
  • Ad view, tap and call records are linked to your anonymous ID, are kept only as long as needed to report totals to instructors, and are deleted on request. The voice counts cannot be linked to you and are kept as totals.

🗑️ Your Rights

  • Delete your data — Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress data. To request deletion of data stored on our servers (including location and diagnostic data), contact us.
  • Opt out of personalized ads — Via your device's ad settings.
  • Refuse or withdraw location access — In your device settings at any time. The whole app keeps working.
  • Deny microphone access — In your device settings at any time.
  • Change your ad choices (Europe) — If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, use the “ad privacy choices” button on the Progress screen at any time.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

  • የሆነ ችግር ሲፈጠር አጭር የምርመራ ሪፖርቶችን መላክ።
  • የማስታወቂያ እይታ፣ የመንካት እና የጥሪ መዝገቦችን እና ከላይ የተገለጹትን ስም-አልባ የድምጽ ብዛቶች ማስቀመጥ

🤝 ሦስተኛ ወገን አገልግሎቶች

  • Supabase — ስም-አልባ ማከማቻ
  • Google AdMob — ማስታወቂያ
  • Google Cloud Speech-to-Text — ድምጽ ማወቂያ
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — ድምጽ ማመንጨት
  • Google Maps Geocoding — መጋጠሚያዎችዎን ወደ ከተማ ስም ይለውጣል
  • ipapi.co — የ IP አድራሻን ወደ ግምታዊ ከተማ ይለውጣል
  • Google Gemini — የ"ለምን?" ማብራሪያዎችን ይጽፋል

👶 የህፃናት ግላዊነት

ይህ መተግበሪያ ከ16 ዓመት በላይ ላሉ ሰዎች ነው። ከ13 ዓመት በታች ህፃናትን የሚመለከቱ መረጃዎች ሆን ብለን አንሰበስብም።

🔐 የመረጃ ደህንነት

በመተግበሪያው እና በሰርቨሮቻችን መካከል የሚላክ ሁሉም መረጃ በ HTTPS/TLS በመጠቀም የተመሰጠረ ነው። ስም-አልባ መለያዎች በዘፈቀደ የሚመነጩ ናቸው እና ስምዎን ወይም የግንኙነት መረጃዎን አልያዙም።

🗓️ መረጃን ለምን ያህል ጊዜ እንደምናስቀምጥ

  • የትምህርት እድገት፣ ስም-አልባ መለያ እና የቦታ መረጃ እንድንሰርዛቸው እስኪጠይቁን ድረስ ይቀመጣሉ።
  • የምርመራ ሪፖርቶች ችግሮችን ለማግኘት እና ለማስተካከል በሚያስፈልገው ጊዜ ብቻ ይቀመጣሉ፣ እና ሲጠየቁ ይሰረዛሉ።
  • ቦታን እምቢ ካሉ፣ ከ IP አድራሻዎ የተገመተ ማንኛውም ከተማ ይሰረዛል እና እንደገና ጥቅም ላይ አይውልም።
  • የድምጽ ቅጂዎች አይቀመጡም።
  • የማስታወቂያ እይታዎች፣ መነካቶች እና የ“አሁን ይደውሉ” መዝገቦች ከእርስዎ ስም-አልባ መለያ ጋር የሚገናኙ ሲሆን፣ ለመንዳት አስተማሪዎች ጠቅላላ ድምርን ሪፖርት ለማድረግ በሚያስፈልገው ጊዜ ብቻ ይቀመጣሉ፣ እና ሲጠየቁ ይሰረዛሉ። የድምጽ ቆጠራዎች ከእርስዎ ጋር ሊገናኙ አይችሉም እና እንደ ጠቅላላ ድምር ይቀመጣሉ።

🗑️ መብቶችዎ

  • መረጃዎን ሰርዙ — መተግበሪያውን ማራገፍ በአካባቢው የተቀመጠውን ሁሉንም የእድገት መረጃ ይሰርዛል። በሰርቨሮቻችን ላይ የተቀመጠውን መረጃ (ቦታ እና የምርመራ መረጃን ጨምሮ) እንዲሰረዝ ለመጠየቅ፣ ያግኙን።
  • ከማስታወቂያ ወጡ — በሞባይል ቅንብሮች።
  • የቦታ መዳረሻን እምቢ ማለት ወይም ማንሳት — በማንኛውም ጊዜ በመሳሪያዎ ቅንብሮች ውስጥ። ሙሉው መተግበሪያ መስራቱን ይቀጥላል።
  • ማይክሮፎን ፈቃድ ሰርዙ — በቅንብሮች በማናቸውም ጊዜ።
  • የእርስዎን የማስታወቂያ ምርጫዎች ይቀይሩ (አውሮፓ) — የአውሮፓ ኢኮኖሚክስ አካባቢ፣ ዩናይትድ ኪንግደም ወይም ስዊዘርላንድ ውስጥ ከሆኑ፣ በማንኛውም ጊዜ በእድገት ማያ ገጽ ላይ ያለውን የ“ማስታወቂያ ምርጫ” ቁልፍን ይጠቀሙ።
